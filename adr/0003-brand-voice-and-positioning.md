# ADR 0003: Brand positioning and voice

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-21

## Context

New solar + heat-pump brand. The founder's brief: the goal is to *support* the grid, not
replace it (off-grid only where no grid exists); carbon reduction is a side benefit, not the
pitch; the value proposition is efficiency, accessibility, reliability and cost advantage.

## Decision

- **Core idea:** "Enerjiyi daha ulaşılabilir, verimli ve ekonomik hâle getiren çözümler."
- **Hero:** "Enerjiniz daha verimli, maliyetiniz daha kontrollü." /
  EN "Your energy, more efficient. Your costs, under control."
- **Mission:** doğru projelendirme ve güvenilir uygulamayla konut ve işletmelerin enerji
  ihtiyacını daha verimli, erişilebilir ve ekonomik biçimde karşılamalarına yardımcı olmak.
- **Voice (5 rules):** technical-but-plain (every kWp/kWh translated to daily life);
  measured, no hype (no "%100 bağımsızlık", no "bedava elektrik"; payback stated as a
  range); benefit-led (every message ties to cost, efficiency, reliability or access;
  environment last, as a by-product); first-person plural, accountable ("projelendiriyoruz,
  kuruyoruz, devreye alıyoruz"), brands and warranties named; calm and local (Bodrum
  climate, holiday vs permanent home realities, no exclamation marks).
- "Neden Luvita" is built on the four values; sustainability is one paragraph on the
  About page, not a page or a nav item. "Dağıtık enerji" may appear as a technical term,
  never as a headline.
- No prices on the site (USD-indexed supply); packages are described by capacity and
  components only.

## Consequences

- Copy reviews check against these rules before merge.
- Slogan shortlist kept in the planning doc; the hero line is the primary slogan.
