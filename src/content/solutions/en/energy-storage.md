## Three needs, three configurations

**1. Hybrid PV (solar + battery).** Surplus daytime generation charges the battery; in the evening you draw from the battery instead of the grid. Self-consumption goes up, and critical loads keep running in an outage.

**2. Backup only.** A battery and hybrid inverter are added to your existing grid connection. It works without solar panels; selected circuits switch to battery automatically the moment the grid drops. The most popular configuration in complexes with frequent outages.

**3. Off-grid.** Where the power line doesn't reach — land, a vineyard house, a boat shed — you build your own grid with solar + battery + (optional) generator support. This is the one exception: otherwise our aim is to support the grid, not to replace it.

## Components

- **Battery:** TommaTech LV LFP lithium modules, from 5 kWh and modular; indoor and outdoor options
- **Inverter:** TommaTech single-/three-phase hybrid with grid–solar–battery management and cloud monitoring
- **Critical-load board:** We decide together at the survey which circuits stay live in an outage

## Sizing approach

We don't size the battery on "bigger is better" but on your evening consumption and the load you want to carry through an outage. In a typical home 5–10 kWh covers most scenarios; more than that stretches the payback. We show this with numbers in the quote.
